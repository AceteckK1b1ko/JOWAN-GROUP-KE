The Process

How On-Location Service Works

A breakdown is a real sequence of events — here's exactly what happens from your call to being back on the road.

1

Call or WhatsApp

Reach our hotline any time, day or night. Tell us your location and what the truck is doing.

2

We Dispatch

A technician and fully equipped mobile unit head straight to your location.

3

On-Site Diagnosis

We connect the diagnostic computer and pinpoint the exact fault before touching a tool.

4

Repair or Recovery

Most issues are fixed on the spot. If not, we arrange towing to our workshop.

5

Back on the Road

You're moving again, with the fault explained and the work warranty-backed.
